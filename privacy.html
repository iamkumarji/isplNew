Privacy Policy

At Innovative Scripts Private Limited, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website and services, including DigiPatra and other software solutions.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide better services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, contact details, and other personal information provided by you when using our services.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and other data collected automatically when you interact with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our services, including DigiPatra.
  • To communicate with you regarding updates, promotions, or support.
  • To enhance user experience by analyzing how our website is accessed and used.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • When we have your consent to do so.
  • When required by law or to comply with legal processes.
  • When necessary to protect our rights, property, or the safety of our users.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website. These cookies help us understand how you use our services and enable us to improve our offerings.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You may also request that we stop using your data for specific purposes, such as marketing communications.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
